Overcome the 3 Core Enterprise Modernization Challenges

Enterprise cloud modernization and monitoring scale face significant friction: unpredictable egress bandwidth costs, complex proprietary alert rules, and fragmented operational views across hybrid environments.

Challenge 01 • Unpredictable Telemetry Costs

Volumetric Data Ingestion & WAN Egress Surcharges

Centralized cloud observability architectures bill for every gigabyte ingested and require transmitting sensitive operational telemetry across public networks. As workloads scale, data egress overages create compounding budget uncertainty.

🛡️ The <dcouplr/> Solution: In‑Perimeter Telemetry Decoupling. Collects, indexes, and evaluates all operational metrics, traces, and logs entirely inside your private cloud perimeter, with zero external bandwidth surcharges.
🔒
Challenge 02 • Rule Translation

Proprietary Monitoring Rule Lock-in

Enterprises maintain thousands of custom monitors, alerting queries, and dashboard configurations across platforms like Datadog, New Relic, and Splunk. Manually translating these rules across platforms during cloud transitions creates months of engineering delay.

⚡ The <dcouplr/> Solution: Multi-Vendor Source-to-Target AST Engine. Automatically parses legacy rule syntax and compiles it into open standards like Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry in seconds.
🌐
Challenge 03 • Fleet Visibility

Fragmented Hybrid Fleet Visibility & Escalation Silos

Operating across bare-metal environments, multi-cloud VPCs, and disparate ITSM tools leads to high MTTR, alert fatigue, and blind spots during critical infrastructure incidents.

🚀 The <dcouplr/> Solution: Unified In‑Perimeter Command & Alert Routing. Centralizes health monitoring with automated alert forwarding to your existing channels, including ServiceNow, Jira, Slack, Teams, and PagerDuty.
